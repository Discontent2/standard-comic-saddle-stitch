# Print Specs

## Target format

- Standard comic trim: **6.625 in × 10.25 in**
- Saddle-stitch binding
- Interior page count must be a multiple of 4
- Suggested first issue: 16 or 24 pages
- Resolution: 300 DPI

## Canvas math

| Area | Inches | Pixels at 300 DPI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trim | 6.625 × 10.25 | 1988 × 3075 |
| Bleed added | +0.125 each edge | +38 px each edge |
| Full bleed | 6.875 × 10.5 | 2063 × 3150 |

The exact width has half-pixels when multiplied by 300, so this kit rounds to practical whole-pixel values.

## Zones

- Bleed: outer 0.125 in. Background art should extend here.
- Trim: where the paper is cut.
- Safe zone: keep important text and faces at least 0.25 in inside trim, preferably 0.375 in.

## Export guidance

- Export single pages, not spreads.
- Use ordered pages.
- Use PDF for printer upload.
- Do not add crop marks unless the printer asks for them.
- Keep a separate proof PDF and final PDF.

## Cover notes

For saddle-stitch, the cover is often supplied as either:

1. A separate front/back/inside-cover PDF, depending on printer requirements.
2. The first and last pages of the single PDF.

Check the printer template before final upload.
